About Me

I'm a game programmer from Palwal, India, with a deep love for the craft. Over the last few years I've gone from building my own runners, shooters and platformers to engineering core systems for commercial games on Steam and mobile.

I care about how a game feels: the snap of an object onto a shelf, a companion that reacts to the room you built, a menu that responds the moment you touch it. I like clean, decoupled C# that the rest of the team can build on, and I like profiling until the framerate is where it should be.

I hold a B.Sc. from Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra, and keep sharpening my Unity 6 skills through courses and side projects. Give me a raw concept, and I'll engineer the mechanics, the feel, and the heart behind it.
